# Privacy Policy — Parkplatz

_Last updated: 2026-07-17_

**Parkplatz** is a single-player sliding-block puzzle game. Your privacy is
simple to describe because the app is designed to need nothing from you.

## What data we collect
**None.** Parkplatz does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

- The app has **no user accounts** and **no login**.
- It contains **no ads** and **no third-party analytics or tracking**.
- It makes **no network requests**; the whole game runs locally on your device.

## Data stored on your device
The game saves your **progress and settings** (completed levels, stars earned,
language, sound/theme options, and any custom levels you create) locally on your
device using the browser/WebView storage (`localStorage`). This data:

- never leaves your device,
- is not accessible to us or anyone else,
- can be erased at any time via the in-app **Reset progress** button or by
  clearing the app's storage / uninstalling the app.

## Permissions
Parkplatz requests no sensitive Android permissions (no location, contacts,
camera, microphone, storage, etc.).

## Children
The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including
children.

## Changes
If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published at the same
location with a new "Last updated" date.
